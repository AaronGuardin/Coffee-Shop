our history

2022

The Beginning

We started with a simple dream: to create a cozy place where people could enjoy fresh, honest food. With a small team and big passion, we opened our doors and welcomed our first guests, hoping to become a warm spot in the neighborhood.

Building Trust

Word spread, and more families, friends, and food lovers began visiting us. We improved our kitchen standards, refined our recipes, and listened closely to customer feedback. This year was all about building trust and consistency.
